# Medical Billing 
New Jersey Pakistan Medical Billing Collaboration

Focused feasibility setup budget and operating design

Date

20 September 2026

Scope

One New Jersey company collaborates with one Pakistan company. The NJ company sells, contracts and manages US relationships. The Pakistan company performs the agreed delivery work.

Executive recommendation

Proceed as a contractual collaboration, not an informal partnership. The NJ company should be the US contracting entity and obtain any NJ third-party billing certification required before marketing or operating as a billing service. The Pakistan company should be a separately registered service provider and HIPAA business-associate subcontractor when it accesses PHI.

Start by securing one to three independent outpatient practices, preferably physical therapy, behavioral health or another specialty already understood by the Pakistan operations lead. Do not launch with patient debt collection, hospital contracts, DME, complex anesthesia or a large fixed office.

Costing approach

New Jersey regulatory and insurance figures in this report are research-based planning estimates and must be checked against current official fees and professional quotations before commitment. Pakistan registration, licensing, workforce and operating costs will be calculated only after official requirements are confirmed and the number, specialty and workload of the first clients are known. No fixed Pakistan operating or marketing budget is approved at this stage.
